# Commercial Documents and PDFs

## Customer-Facing Quotation PDF

The quotation PDF is the primary external commercial document. It should include:

- Company name, address, phone, email, website, logo, and GST number.
- Customer name, company, billing address, and delivery/site address.
- Project reference and quotation number.
- Revision number and quotation date.
- Valid-until date.
- Line items with location, reference, system, color, glass, width, height, quantity, area, rate, and amount.
- Discount, installation, freight, lifting, taxes, and grand total.
- Payment terms.
- Quotation terms and conditions.
- Notes where commercially approved.

## Production Documents

Production-facing documents should be treated as controlled workshop records.

Expected outputs:

- Optimized cutting list.
- Hardware summary.
- Glass schedule.
- Optimization summary.
- Job detail record.

## Document Control

Commercial use requires document control rules:

- Quotation numbers must be unique.
- Revisions must be visible.
- Sent and accepted statuses must be tracked.
- Accepted quotation PDFs should be archived.
- Production documents should refer to a locked or accepted project.
- Any manual changes after acceptance should create a revision or documented approval.

## PDF Validation Checklist

Before launch:

- Generate a quotation for a simple single-window project.
- Generate a quotation for a multi-line project with multiple systems.
- Verify taxes and grand total manually.
- Verify company branding.
- Verify page breaks and table overflow.
- Verify terms and payment instructions.
- Generate production documents from the same project.
- Compare cutting list output with formula expectations.

## Commercial Risk Areas

- A PDF total that differs from screen totals creates customer disputes.
- Missing tax registration details can create accounting issues.
- Production documents generated from unconfirmed dimensions can cause fabrication waste.
- Terms and conditions should be reviewed by the business owner or legal adviser.
